# Phase 4L inquiry-draft validation

The Phase 4K drafts were retained unchanged.  Two revision-2 drafts were created solely to make blocker, rule, field/product, and operational tracking explicit.

`REQ-SLX-001` maps to `B-SLX-GTI-001`, `B-SLX-PROV-002`, and `B-SLX-CAL-003` (R03, R05, R06).  It identifies observed SDD2 L1 daily light curve and GTI companion products; asks every GTI, compatibility, and CALDB question in answerable form; requests governing revision, citation, and scope; and explicitly permits “no formal policy exists” as an answer.

`REQ-HL-002` maps to `B-HL-TIME-004`, `B-HL-QUALITY-005`, and `B-HL-VERSION-006` (R07, R08, R13, R14).  It identifies observed HEL1OS L1 detector/band light curves and V111/V211 counterparts; asks for time authority, discrepancy behavior, HK/quality definitions, thresholds/actions, and exact version treatment; and requests governing revision, citation, and scope.

Neither draft assumes an endpoint convention, threshold, timestamp precedence, calibration method, or version rule.  The official recipient/channel is intentionally not invented: the user must identify or verify it through the authorized official channel before a request may be recorded as sent.
